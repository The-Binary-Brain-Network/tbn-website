Questions

Pricing questions, answered.

How much does TBN software cost?
Folio costs ₹6,500 per property per year. Pass costs ₹7,500 per billing computer per year, with every outlet run from that computer included. Slate costs ₹12,000 per property per year, not per room and not per device. Each has a 15-day free trial of the complete software.
Is it a subscription?
It is an annual licence, not a monthly SaaS plan. You pay once a year; there are no per-seat, per-room or per-device charges and no paid add-on tiers. Renewal is never automatic — we contact you before it is due and you confirm.
What is included in the price?
The complete product, installation, onboarding, staff training, updates for the year and a direct WhatsApp support line. Nothing is held back for a higher tier.
Why does Pass charge per computer?
Each Pass installation is its own billing station, and the licence is bound to that computer. Everything you run from it — any number of outlets, each with its own GSTIN and bill series — is included.
Is there a discount for referrals?
Yes. Refer another business that buys a licence and you get ₹1,500 off, credited immediately rather than held for renewal. It stacks across referrals. The business you refer pays the standard price.
Do I need to buy new hardware?
Usually not. Every product runs on a Windows 10/11 or Linux PC you already own, with a standard thermal receipt printer. Slate adds Android phones you already have.
What happens to my data if I stop paying?
It stays on your computer. Every product exports your data — Folio as 16 CSV files, Pass and Slate to Excel and PDF — so leaving never means losing your records.
