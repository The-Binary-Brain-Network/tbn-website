Feature List

Every screen, every function.

01

Orders & Tables

Order typesDine-in, takeaway and delivery, all from one screen with no mode-switching ceremony.
Table floor planVisual layout with sections — AC Room, Garden, Rooftop — and colour-coded status: available, occupied, reserved, cleaning.
Quick Order EntryKeyboard-driven type-ahead search for punching orders fast at a busy counter.
Merge tablesCombine several tables onto one bill when a group pushes tables together.
Transfer orderMove an order to a different table without re-punching a single item.
Hold ordersPark an order and come back to it.
Cancel with reasonCancellations are recorded with a reason, not silently deleted.
Complementary ordersComp an order with a reason logged, so it shows correctly in reports rather than distorting sales.
Order statusPlaced → KOT sent → preparing → ready → served → billed.
Order numberingAuto-generated and dated, so every order is traceable.
Customer lookupFind a returning customer by phone as you take the order.
Online-orders inboxA single queue for incoming delivery orders — accept, reject, and push straight into the kitchen.
02

Kitchen

Kitchen Display SystemIncluded in the licence — no separate screen fee, no add-on tier.
Station filteringEach screen shows only its own station's tickets, so tandoor isn't reading Chinese orders.
Tap to advanceMove a ticket through preparing and ready with one touch.
Age colouringTickets warm at 8 minutes and go red at 15, so nothing sits forgotten at the pass.
Kitchen stationsDefine your stations and map menu categories to them.
Station-wise KOT routingA category's tickets print at that station's own printer automatically. Unmapped categories fall back to the default.
KOT printingAuto-numbered per order, printed silently to any 80mm thermal printer.
Separate printersDifferent printers for kitchen tickets and customer bills, configured once.
03

Billing & Payments

GST billingCGST, SGST and IGST calculated and shown correctly, with GSTIN and FSSAI printed on the bill.
Tax modesOne flat rate across the menu, or a rate set per item where slabs differ.
Split billSettle one order as several separately-numbered invoices — by equal share or by picking items.
Split paymentsCash, UPI, card and loyalty points against a single bill, each recorded with its own reference.
Static UPI QRWorks with any UPI app, needs no gateway account and no API key. Just your UPI ID.
Razorpay QRSingle-use QR per bill with the amount pre-filled, and automatic payment confirmation so the counter isn't waiting on the customer to say "done".
PhonePeSupported as a payment gateway option.
Cash change calculatorColour-coded so the cashier reads it at a glance.
Bill voidVoided with a reason, the staff member and the timestamp all recorded.
Bill adjustmentsRefunds and corrections recorded properly rather than overwriting history.
Discount ceilings per roleAn owner can discount fully; a manager, cashier and waiter each have their own limit. Nobody hands out more than their role allows.
Bill numberingSequential with a configurable prefix, per outlet, audit-safe.
Auto-print on paymentOptional — the bill prints itself the moment payment lands.
04

Menu

CategoriesNested, colour-coded, with item counts and sort order.
VariantsHalf and full, small and large — one item, several sizes, each priced independently.
ModifiersExtra cheese, no onion — free or priced, applied at order time.
Veg / non-veg / eggMarked with the standard colour dots your customers expect.
Cost price trackingStored per item, so margin is real rather than estimated.
Per-item tax rate0%, 5%, 12% or 18% set per item where the slabs differ.
Availability toggleMark an item sold out in one tap; it greys out at the counter instantly.
Excel importBulk-import your menu from a spreadsheet, with a downloadable template.
Import from a photoPoint a camera at your printed menu and let it read the items in, then review before committing.
05

Inventory & Recipes

Stock itemsUnits in kg, g, litre, ml, piece or dozen, with cost per unit and a low-stock threshold.
Stock transactionsPurchases, adjustments and wastage, each with vendor and notes.
Low-stock alertsFire at your configured threshold, so you find out before service, not during it.
RecipesLink a menu item to its ingredients with a quantity per serving.
Automatic deductionSelling a dish deducts its ingredients from stock on its own. Optional — switch it off if you'd rather not.
Excel importBulk-import inventory from a spreadsheet.
Stock logEvery movement, kept for reconciliation.
06

Multiple Outlets

Per-outlet identityEach outlet keeps its own name, address, GSTIN, FSSAI and phone.
Separate bill numberingEach outlet has its own prefix and counter. Two outlets sharing one sequence would issue duplicate invoice numbers, which is a GST problem — Pass prevents it.
Central master menuOne menu shared across outlets, so a price change lands everywhere at once.
Per-outlet operationsOrders, tables, sections, inventory, expenses and staff are kept separate per outlet.
Outlet switcherChange which outlet you're working in from inside the app.
LicensingUnlimited outlets on a single licence at current pricing.
07

Staff & Payroll

RolesOwner, manager, cashier, waiter and kitchen, with 14 granular permissions between them.
PIN loginA short PIN per staff member — no passwords to manage or forget.
Manager overrideSensitive actions ask for a manager's PIN rather than blocking the shift.
AttendanceMonthly grid with keyboard shortcuts for present, absent, half day and leave, plus check-in tracking.
Salary sheetsComputed monthly from attendance, salary type and advances — gross, deductions, net. Exports to Excel.
Salary advancesRecorded and deducted automatically at payroll.
Excel importBulk-import your staff list.
Lock screenLock the till between shifts without closing the app.
08

Customers & Loyalty

Phone-keyed databaseOne record per customer, found instantly by number.
Loyalty pointsEarned on spend and redeemable as a payment mode at the counter.
Visit trackingVisit count, total spend and average per visit.
Order historyEverything that customer has ordered, on their record.
ProfilesName, address, area, city, birthday, email and notes — so a regular gets treated like one.
WhatsApp notificationsTell a delivery customer their order is ready, pre-formatted, in one click.
09

Reports & Intelligence

DashboardToday's revenue, active orders, average bill value and cash-versus-UPI collection, updating live.
Daily summaryRevenue, bill count and payment split for the day.
Sales reportsAny date range, with a daily breakdown.
Item-wiseQuantity sold, revenue and average price per item.
GST reportMonthly, bill-level, with the CGST/SGST/IGST breakdown your accountant needs.
Profit & lossGross revenue, categorised expenses and net margin.
Expense trackerTen categories seeded on setup — rent, electricity, raw materials, salaries and more.
Business insightsToday read against yesterday, with specific things to do about it.
Sales forecastA seven-day projection built from your own trading history.
Menu pricing advisorFlags items that underperform and suggests what to change.
Marketing generatorReady-to-send WhatsApp copy for promotions, festivals and new launches.
ExportEvery report to Excel and PDF.
Role-gatedRestrict reports to the staff who should see them.
10

Infrastructure

Offline-firstEvery core operation runs with the internet unplugged. Nothing waits on a server.
Multi-terminalCashier, kitchen and waiter stations on your own WiFi, all sharing one live view.
Zero-config discoveryTerminals find the host automatically. No IP addresses to type, no router settings to learn.
Offline queueA terminal that drops off the network keeps taking orders and catches up when it returns.
Local backupsAutomatic, with retention and a restore path.
Optional cloud replicationAn off-site copy of your trading data, entirely opt-in.
Auto-updatesDownloaded in the background, installed on quit — never mid-service.
LicensingSigned licence key, verified offline, with optional hardware binding.
ThemesDark and light, switched instantly.
Guided tourAn in-app walkthrough on first run, plus a built-in FAQ.

Technical Specification

What it needs, and what it runs on.

PlatformWindows desktop. Per-user install — no admin rights required.
HardwareAny Windows PC the restaurant already owns. No server to buy.
Printing80mm thermal printers over USB, network or the Windows default. Silent printing with no dialog. Separate printers for kitchen tickets, bills and each kitchen station.
InternetNot required. Core billing, ordering, kitchen display and reporting all run fully offline.
DatabaseEmbedded local database on your own machine.
BackupsAutomatic local backups with retention and a restore path. Optional cloud replication for an off-site copy.
Multi-terminalOne host terminal plus unlimited clients on the same network, discovered automatically. Clients queue offline and sync on reconnect.
OutletsUnlimited outlets on one licence at current pricing, each with its own GSTIN, FSSAI and bill numbering.
SecurityPIN authentication with hashed storage, role-based permissions enforced across the app, and a signed licence verified offline.
PaymentsStatic UPI QR with no gateway account needed, plus Razorpay and PhonePe. Card and cash recorded with references.
GSTFlat or per-item rates at 0, 5, 12 and 18 percent, with CGST/SGST/IGST computed and printed.
SetupGuided wizard, then terminal setup. Most restaurants are billing the same day, including menu import.
UpdatesAutomatic — downloaded in the background, applied on quit.
SupportDirect WhatsApp line. Not a ticketing queue.
