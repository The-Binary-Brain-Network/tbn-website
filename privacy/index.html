Privacy policy

What this website and our software collect, why, and what you can do about it — in plain language.

Who we are

This website, tbn.tbbn.in, is run by The Binary Brain Network ("TBN", "we"), the software division of The Big Brand Network, Mumbai, Maharashtra, India. For anything in this policy, write to tbn@tbbn.in or WhatsApp +91 88790 70732. We act as the data fiduciary for the personal data described here, under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

What this website collects

When you contact us

The site has no forms. If you message us on WhatsApp, call or email, we receive what you send — usually your name, phone number, business name and your question — and use it only to reply, arrange a trial or demo, and provide support. Pre-filled WhatsApp messages include a short reference to the page you came from (for example ref: /pass/) so we know what you were reading; you can delete it before sending.

Measurement and advertising

We use Google Tag Manager to load measurement and advertising tools. Until you choose "Accept" on the cookie bar, these run without cookies or identifiers (Google's consent mode), and advertising tools do not load at all. If you accept, the following may set cookies or similar identifiers:

  • Google Analytics — which pages are read and which downloads are started.
  • Google Ads, Meta (Facebook and Instagram), Microsoft Advertising and LinkedIn — whether a visit that came from one of our ads went on to download a trial or contact us, so we can measure and improve the ads.
  • Microsoft Clarity — anonymised heatmaps and session replays that show where a page confuses people. Clarity masks text you type.

These providers process data under their own privacy policies and may do so outside India. We do not sell personal data, and we do not use it to make decisions about you.

Choices you have

You can decline on the cookie bar and the site works exactly the same. To change your mind, clear this site's data in your browser and the bar will appear again. Browser settings and ad-blockers also block these tools.

What our software collects

Folio, Pass and Slate are installed on your own computer and keep your business data there — your bills, guests, menu, staff and reports are not sent to us.

  • Licence and trial activation. When you start a trial or activate a licence, the software sends your business name, phone number and an identifier derived from your computer to our licence server, so that each computer gets one trial and a key cannot be copied to another machine. We are notified by email of each new trial so we can offer help.
  • Licence checks. When online, the software periodically checks whether its key has been revoked.
  • Updates. The software checks our GitHub release page for new versions.
  • Crash reports. If the software crashes it can send an error report with personal details removed. You can switch this off in the app's settings.
  • Features you switch on. Some optional features use the internet on your instruction — sending a bill or alert on WhatsApp, emailing a report from your own email account, or confirming a UPI payment with Razorpay or PhonePe. That data goes to the service you chose, not to us.

Each product also includes its own privacy notice inside the app.

How long we keep it

Conversations and customer records are kept while we have a business relationship with you and for as long afterwards as tax and accounting law requires. Licence and trial records are kept for the life of the licence plus that period. Analytics data is kept for the retention period set in each tool, at most 14 months for Google Analytics.

Your rights

You may ask us to tell you what personal data we hold about you, correct it, or erase it where we are not required to keep it; withdraw consent at any time; and nominate someone to exercise these rights on your behalf. Write to tbn@tbbn.in; we reply within 30 days. If you are not satisfied, you may approach the Data Protection Board of India.
