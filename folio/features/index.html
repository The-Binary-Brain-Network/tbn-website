Feature List

Every screen, every function.

01

Tabs & Orders

Room tabsOne open tab per room, opened at check-in with guest name, phone and check-in date. Running total always visible.
Payment modes per tabSettle on checkout, daily, or per order — set per tab to match how that guest is paying.
Order sourcesOrder from a partner restaurant, your own kitchen, or split a single order across both.
Menu searchType-ahead search grouped by restaurant, with a cart and quantity stepping.
Custom itemsAdd a one-off item that isn't on the menu, without polluting the menu.
Order statusReceived → preparing → ready → delivered, tracked per item and rolled up per order.
Markup modesNone, a percentage markup, or a fixed amount added — applied per item.
Overdue flagA tab unpaid past three days marks itself visually, so nothing ages quietly.
Tab statesOpen, due, settled, written off — each visually distinct on the board.
Duplicate protectionA room can't accidentally end up with two open tabs.
02

Billing

Consolidated billOne bill across a date range with optional discount and notes. Preview before printing.
Two copiesCustomer copy shows items and totals; hotel copy shows cost against markup per source, so you see the margin.
Print optionsPrint both copies, customer only, hotel only, or share to WhatsApp.
Bill numberingSequential with your own prefix and counter. Audit-safe — numbering never resets mid-year.
ReprintAny past bill, any time, no time limit, identical to the original.
RectificationFully edit a finalised bill — guest, period, items, discount, GST. Creates a new versioned bill linked to the original with a reason logged. The original is never destroyed.
Cost-only overrideAdjust the cost side without touching the guest bill, then auto-reprint the hotel copy with the new margin.
RefundsRecorded against the bill with a full trail, and automatically deducted from day-close revenue.
Bulk billingSelect several tabs and close them onto one combined bill — for group checkouts and corporate stays.
Rupee roundingOptional, with the adjustment shown transparently as its own "Round Off" line.
03

Dues & Pay-Later

On-account billsA corporate guest signs and leaves; the room frees up for the next guest while the bill stays tracked.
Revenue recognitionDues are not counted as revenue until actually collected, so your reports show cash reality.
Aging buckets0–7, 8–30, 31–60 and 60+ days, with total outstanding at a glance.
SearchBy guest name, room number or bill number.
Part paymentPay against a due in instalments, with balance tracked.
Bad-debt write-offWrite off an uncollectable bill; GST on it is reversed automatically so your filings stay accurate.
CSV exportFull dues list with totals and aging summary, for your accountant.
04

Menu, Restaurants & Inventory

Partner restaurantsAdd the restaurants you source from, with address, phone and display order.
Menu itemsSource, cost price, selling price, unit (half, full, plate, pieces), category, GST rate and availability.
Unit disambiguationTwo items with the same name are shown distinctly by unit, so staff never pick the wrong one.
CSV / TSV importImport your menu from any spreadsheet, with column mapping and a preview. Unknown restaurant names are created for you.
Inventory itemsStock levels, minimum thresholds, cost per unit and category.
Stock transactionsPurchases, usage and adjustments, with vendor recorded.
Recipe linkingLink a menu item to an inventory item so stock deducts automatically when it sells.
Low-stock alertsSurfaced at day close and shift handover, where someone will actually see them.
05

Reports

Owner's daily reportGenerated and printed automatically every day without anyone asking, and frozen so the numbers can't drift later. Reprint or recompute any past day.
Revenue summaryCollected revenue on a cash basis, food cost, gross profit, expenses, inventory, bad debt, refunds, dues added and net profit.
Comprehensive reportFinancials, outstanding dues with aging, room-wise performance, top items, expenses, write-offs and refunds — exportable as one CSV covering all eight sections.
Room-wiseRevenue by room, with manually-overridden rows flagged.
Item-wiseTop sellers and thinnest margins.
ExpensesIngredient, kitchen supply, food purchase and miscellaneous, with vendor and date-range filtering.
Day closeOnce per calendar day, PIN-gated, printed, and locks the date. Guards against running an earlier date than the last close.
Shift handoverPulls today's payments by method, you enter counted cash and UPI, and it prints a signed report showing any difference.
06

Security & Data

Database encryptionAES-256 with the key derived from the machine itself — copy the file to another PC and it's unreadable.
Manager PIN4–8 digits, heavily hashed, protecting day close, refunds, write-offs, rectification and factory reset.
Hourly backups48 retained — two full days of history — stored outside the install folder so updates never touch them.
Automatic recoveryIf the database is damaged, Folio finds the newest good backup and restores it on startup, keeping the damaged file aside.
LicensingHardware-bound licence key, verified offline. Online revocation and extension checked periodically.
Guarded factory resetTakes a mandatory backup first, needs your PIN and a typed confirmation, and keeps your setup data.
Crash reportingAutomatic and anonymised, so we can fix a problem before you report it. Can be switched off.
DiagnosticsA built-in health check with a one-click error report to support.
07

Day-to-Day

Keyboard shortcutsJump between screens, open search and bring up help without touching the mouse.
In-app guideSearchable help covering every feature — a new staff member isn't blocked waiting on you.
ThemesDark and light. The dark one matters at 2am.
Printer alignmentA printed ruler page for fine-tuning margins on stubborn printers.
Auto-updatesDownload in the background, install on restart. Never interrupts a shift.
Setup wizardSeven guided steps — hotel details, logo, rooms, partner restaurants, PIN. Under two minutes.

Technical Specification

What it needs, and what it runs on.

Version3.10.0
PlatformWindows desktop. Runs as a standard per-user install — no admin password needed.
HardwareAny Windows PC the property already owns. No server, no new hardware.
Printing80mm thermal receipt printers, printed silently with no dialog. Works with any printer installed on Windows. Includes a raw fallback for cheap printers that ignore the standard cut command.
InternetNot required. Every core operation works fully offline. Internet is used only for updates, crash reports and the WhatsApp share link.
DatabaseEmbedded local database, AES-256 encrypted, bound to the machine.
BackupsHourly, 48 retained, stored outside the install folder, with automatic corruption recovery on startup.
Multi-terminalSingle machine. Folio is designed as one install for the front desk.
LicensingOne licence per property, hardware-bound, verified offline.
UpdatesAutomatic — downloaded in the background, applied on restart.
GSTOptional CGST + SGST with a per-item rate override. Configurable rates and GSTIN on every bill.
LegalEULA, Terms, Privacy and Liability bundled and readable inside the app.
SupportDirect WhatsApp line. Not a ticketing queue.
