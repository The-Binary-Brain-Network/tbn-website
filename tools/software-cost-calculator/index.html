Typical prices in India.

How much does hotel management software cost in India?
Cloud hotel PMS in India is commonly priced at ₹50 to ₹200 per room per month, or ₹10,000 to ₹25,000 a year for flat-priced products. Slate is ₹12,000 per property per year regardless of room count.
How much does restaurant billing software cost in India?
Cloud restaurant POS commonly runs ₹800 to ₹2,500 a month; self-hosted licences are typically ₹6,000 to ₹9,000 a year. Pass is ₹7,500 per billing computer per year.
