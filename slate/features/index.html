Feature List

Every screen, every function.

01

Front Desk & Bookings

Live room boardThe home screen. Every room, colour-coded by occupied, vacant, dirty, arriving today or out of order.
Walk-in check-inGuest details, ID capture, room assignment and folio opened in one guided flow.
Advance bookingsFuture reservations with a booking calendar view.
Today's arrivalsWho's expected, at a glance, with per-room arrival lookup.
Room movesChange a guest's room mid-stay, with available rooms shown and the folio following them.
Checkout previewSee the complete total before committing, then settle.
Hourly & slot staysConfigurable time slots with a live quote per room, in-house hourly guest tracking, and negotiable overstay charges.
Group bookingsOne reservation across many rooms, with a master folio and per-room allocation.
Room typesDefined with base rate and maximum occupancy.
Room historyEvery stay a given room has had — who, when, what they paid.
No-show handlingMarked automatically at night audit and reported.
02

Rates & Availability

Rate plansDefine your plans once and apply them across bookings.
SeasonsSeasonal pricing that applies itself on the right dates.
Rate calendarPer-date rates, visible and editable across the month.
Rate gridRoom types against dates in one view.
Preview before publishingSee what a rate change actually does before it goes live.
Live quoteInstant quote for a room and date range at the desk.
Occupancy forecastProjected occupancy from confirmed bookings.
03

Folios & Billing

Guest folioOne running account per stay — room charges, F&B, laundry and incidentals, all itemised.
Post chargesAdd any charge to a folio at any point in the stay.
Record paymentsCash, UPI, card or bank transfer, each with its own reference.
Discounts with approvalAbove a percentage you set, a discount needs a manager. Below it, the desk just gets on with it.
Charge voidsVoided with a reason logged. Nothing disappears silently.
GST invoicingConfigurable GST bands for room tariff, so your property's slabs are applied correctly.
UPI QR on the billPrinted on the invoice — guest scans and pays without going to the desk.
Branded PDF invoicesYour logo, header note, footer note, terms, bank details, accent colour and title.
Email the invoiceSent from your own mailbox — connect your SMTP in Settings, with a test button. Optionally automatic at checkout.
Billing registerEvery invoice, searchable, with sequential numbering and your own prefix.
Advance paymentsRecorded against future bookings, with balance due shown at check-in.
04

Guests & CRM

Guest searchBy phone or name, with full stay history and total spend.
VIP flagMarked once, visible to everyone at the desk.
BlacklistSo a problem guest isn't quietly re-admitted by the night shift.
Internal notesPer guest, staff-only.
ID & photo captureCamera, file upload, or a Windows flatbed scanner driven directly from the app.
Guest segmentsGrouped for reporting and follow-up.
Pre-arrival check-inSend a secure link, the guest fills in their own details and documents, and your desk reviews and applies or rejects it. Links can be revoked.
05

Housekeeping & Maintenance

Room statusClean, dirty, in progress, inspected and out of order.
Auto-flag on checkoutA departing room marks itself dirty; inspection clears it.
Task assignmentPer housekeeper, with printable daily lists.
Mobile housekeeping viewA dedicated phone screen so the floor updates status where they are, not on a clipboard.
Arrival priorityRooms with a guest arriving today are flagged for turning first.
Maintenance logOut-of-order rooms with reason, expected return and resolution tracking.
Productivity reportingRooms turned per staff member, in the manager's report.

Compliance

C-Form registerFull foreigner registration record, auto-populated from check-in data.
Document attachmentsPassport and visa scans attached to the C-Form entry.
Guest photo on Form CPrinted on the form itself, as expected.
Submission trackingMark entries submitted so you know what's outstanding.
CSV exportFor FRRO submission.
FRRO settingsYour user ID, accommodation type, grade, capacity and owner details, stored once and reprinted on every form.
GSTR-1 exportYour return data, straight out of the app.
Tally XML exportHand your accountant a file, not a shoebox.
GST reportBill-level breakdown for the period.
Audit logA record of who did what, so a discrepancy has a trail.
Card data refusedSlate actively rejects card numbers pasted into reference fields, keeping your property out of card-compliance scope by design.
07

Staff, Attendance & Payroll

Six rolesOwner, manager, receptionist, housekeeping, F&B and accounts — enforced on the server, not just hidden from the menu.
PIN loginA short PIN per staff member, securely stored.
Brute-force protectionRepeated wrong PINs lock out on a rising delay, but never so long that a receptionist is stuck mid-shift.
Staff recordsAdd, edit, deactivate and reactivate, with PIN reset.
AttendanceDaily marking, bulk present, and full history — with automatic fill so a missed midnight doesn't break the month.
PayrollComputed monthly from attendance, salary type and advances.
Salary advancesRecorded and deducted at payroll.

F&B sales summaryFood and beverage revenue reported separately from rooms.

11

Devices, Data & Security

Runs on the front deskThe desk PC holds the data and serves every other device on your network.
Android appScan a QR at reception and the phone pairs itself. Holds no data of its own — it talks to the desk.
Installable web appAny phone, tablet or PC on the network can install Slate to its home screen.
Zero-config discoveryDevices find the desk automatically. No IP addresses, no router settings.
Secure connection for camerasA secure local connection so ID and passport capture works properly on phones, including iPhones.
Firewall handledThe installer creates the network rule for you.
Daily automatic backups30 retained, taken safely while the system is live so a backup is never a half-written file.
Recoverable restoreRestoring stages the change and keeps the database it replaced, so even a bad restore can be undone.
Download backupsKeep your own copy off the machine.
Guest data stays localGuest ID numbers, ID photos and C-Form entries are never sent to the cloud, by design.
Optional cloud syncBookings, bills and expenses only, and only if you switch it on.
LicensingSigned licence key bound to your machine, verified offline.
DiagnosticsA built-in health screen showing version, modules, database and logs.
Auto-updatesApplied on quit, never mid-shift, with progress shown in the app.

Technical Specification

What it needs, and what it runs on.

Version0.4.0
PlatformWindows desktop for the front desk, plus an Android app and an installable web app for every other device.
HardwareAny reasonably modern Windows PC. No server to buy, no cloud subscription in the middle.
DevicesUnlimited phones, tablets and PCs on the same network. No per-device licence.
Android appDistributed from your own front desk by QR rather than the Play Store, because Slate is designed to work with a paired desk on your network.
InternetNot required. Bookings, check-in, folios, billing, housekeeping, reports and backups all run offline. Internet is used only for optional WhatsApp, invoice email and updates.
DatabaseEmbedded local database on the front-desk machine.
BackupsDaily and automatic, 30 retained, taken safely while live. Restore is staged and keeps the replaced database.
ScanningWindows flatbed scanners driven from the app, with camera and file upload as alternatives.
SecurityPIN authentication with hashed storage, six server-enforced roles, brute-force lockout, and an audit log.
PaymentsCash, UPI, card and bank transfer recorded with references, plus a UPI QR on the invoice. Card numbers are actively refused.
ComplianceC-Form / FRRO register with CSV export, GST invoicing with configurable room bands, GSTR-1 export and Tally XML export.
Property sizeDesigned for roughly 5 to 100 rooms.
SetupGuided seven-step wizard, then an in-app tutorial on the room board.
UpdatesAutomatic — downloaded in the background, applied on quit.
SupportDirect WhatsApp line. Not a ticketing queue.
