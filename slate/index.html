Our Story

Built by hoteliers, for hoteliers.

Slate wasn't drawn on a whiteboard by product managers who had never worked a front desk. Our family has been in Indian hospitality for three generations — running and operating properties, managing check-in queues at midnight, filing C-Forms for foreign guests, chasing housekeeping across three floors.

Somewhere along the way we became technologists too. And what we kept seeing was the same trap — hotel software either priced for a chain of 100 or held together with spreadsheets. Nothing in between worked the way an Indian mid-range property actually operates.

So we built our own. Every module in Slate answers a question one of us asked as an operator first — long before we were writing code for it.

What It Does

Everything from booking to checkout.

Slate covers the full lifecycle of a hotel operation, including the compliance and staff bits that most PMS quietly ignore. All under one flat annual licence.

01

Front Desk Operations

Every arrival, every departure, every room, on one screen.

  • Bookings management — new reservations, edits, cancellations, no-shows, walk-ins
  • Room board — real-time grid of every room with colour-coded status (occupied, vacant, dirty, check-in today, out-of-order)
  • Guided check-in flow — ID capture, room assignment, folio open, key handover, all in one screen
  • Check-out with folio settlement — auto-computes stay total, F&B, incidentals; supports part-payment and dues
  • Guest master — phone-based lookup, stay history, preferences, address book
  • Multi-room and group bookings — one reservation, many rooms, shared folio option
02

Billing & Folios

Guest folios that survive the audit. Bills the guest will pay without argument.

  • Guest folio per stay — every room charge, F&B posting, laundry, incidental itemised
  • GST-compliant tax invoice — 12% or 18% based on room tariff, with correct CGST/SGST/IGST split
  • Split billing — separate bills for company vs personal charges on the same stay
  • Multi-payment — cash, UPI, card, bank transfer on the same folio, per line
  • Advance payment tracking against future bookings; balance due at check-in
  • Bill reprint anytime, versioned corrections with reason logged
  • GSTIN & FSSAI on every bill, sequential numbering with configurable prefix
03

Housekeeping

The floor knows what to clean before the front desk needs it.

  • Room status tracker — clean, dirty, in-progress, inspected, out-of-order
  • Auto-flag rooms as dirty on check-out, cleared on inspection
  • Task assignment per housekeeper with printable daily lists
  • Priority flags for expected check-ins today
  • Maintenance log for out-of-order rooms with reason and ETA
04

Compliance

The paperwork Indian hotels legally must do. Handled.

  • C-Form generation for foreign guests — auto-populated from check-in data, ready to submit
  • Passport, visa, PAN, Aadhaar capture during check-in
  • GST invoicing with correct rate slabs per stay
  • Sequential bill numbering — audit-safe, no silent overwrites
  • Bundled legal docs in-app: EULA, Terms, Privacy, Liability
  • Data-export for GST returns, F&B reconciliation, income statements
05

Staff & Payroll

Team management on the same machine as the front desk.

  • Role-based access — Owner, Manager, Front Desk, Housekeeping, Kitchen
  • 4-digit PIN authentication, manager PIN override for sensitive actions
  • Monthly attendance grid with P/A/H/L keyboard shortcuts
  • Auto-calculated payroll — gross, advances, deductions, net pay; export to Excel
  • Salary advances with deduction tracking
  • Shift handover reports at day-close with cash reconciliation
06

Reports, Ops & WhatsApp

Numbers the owner reads, plus the last-mile guest touch.

  • Occupancy report — daily, weekly, monthly with ADR & RevPAR
  • Revenue report — rooms vs F&B vs others, per source
  • Expense tracker with categories, exportable to Excel/PDF
  • Day-close & night-audit — auto-printed, cash reconciled
  • WhatsApp bill share — one click, pre-filled with guest phone
  • WhatsApp booking confirmations and check-in reminders
  • Owner PWA dashboard — remote view of key metrics on phone (optional)

Answers

Questions hotels ask before signing.

What actually happens during the 14-day free trial?
We install the full product on your front-desk machine, run the onboarding wizard with you, and train your team in a live walkthrough — all included. You use every feature for 14 days, exactly as a paid customer would. If Slate doesn't earn its place, you owe nothing and we uninstall it. No card up front.
Does Slate really work offline?
Yes — 100% of core operations (bookings, check-in, folios, billing, housekeeping, reports, backups) work fully offline. Internet is only needed for optional WhatsApp notifications, silent product updates and the owner PWA dashboard. A property with a dead connection can operate for a full shift without missing anything.
Do you handle C-Form for foreign guests?
Yes. Slate auto-populates the C-Form from check-in data (passport, visa, arrival, address) and produces a ready-to-submit form. Foreign-guest data is captured during check-in and stored securely on the local database.
Does it work with my existing hotel software or Folio?
Slate is a complete PMS on its own — it doesn't need another hotel system underneath. It does pair natively with Folio: if you already run Folio for room-service billing, Slate slots in for bookings, folios and front desk. Both share a support line and pricing model.
How many rooms is Slate designed for?
Slate is designed for boutique to mid-range properties — roughly 5 to 100 rooms. Below that, it may be more than you need (look at Folio for guest houses instead). Above that, we'll want to talk about your specific integration needs.
What happens on renewal?
Renewals are billed at the standard flat ₹9,500 per property per year, regardless of how you got in. No auto-charge — we contact you before renewal and you confirm. No usage tiers or surprise hikes.
Can I get my data out?
Yes. Rooms, bookings, guests, folios, staff and reports export to Excel and PDF directly from the app. The underlying database is standard SQLite, portable to anywhere. No data-ransom situation, ever.
Who builds Slate?
Slate is built by The Binary Brain Network, the software division of The Big Brand Network — a Mumbai-based full-service brand ecosystem operating across Delhi NCR and pan-India. Same team behind Folio and Pass.
