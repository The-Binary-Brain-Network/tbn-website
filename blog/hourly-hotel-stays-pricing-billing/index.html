Hourly hotel stays: pricing, selling and billing short stays.

Transit passengers, day-long business meetings, families waiting for an evening train: hourly stays are a real and growing market in Indian cities. Selling them well means treating a room as something that can turn over more than once a day — without losing track of it.

Short answer

To sell hourly stays, define fixed slots (for example 3, 6 and 12 hours) with their own prices, set an overstay rule, block enough time between slots for housekeeping, and record each stay with the same guest identification and GST invoicing as a night stay. GST is charged at the rate for the value actually charged for the unit.

Who buys hourly stays

  • Transit travellers near airports and railway stations with a long layover.
  • Business visitors who need a room to freshen up or work between meetings.
  • Families in the city for a hospital appointment, an exam or a function.
  • Drivers and crew resting between shifts.

For a hotel with daytime vacancy, a room sold for six hours in the afternoon and again at night is extra revenue from the same inventory.

Design your slots

Do not sell "any number of hours". Fixed slots are easier to price, easier for staff to explain and easier to schedule housekeeping around. A common pattern:

SlotExample price (night rate ₹2,400)Logic
3 hours₹1,000Highest per-hour price — the room still needs a full clean
6 hours₹1,500Most popular for day use
12 hours₹2,000Close to a night; leaves room for one more slot

Shorter slots carry a higher hourly price because the cleaning cost is the same whether the guest stayed three hours or twelve.

Overstay rules

Decide before it happens:

  • A grace period (for example 15 minutes).
  • An hourly overstay charge after that.
  • The point at which an overstay converts to the next slot or a full night.
  • Whether the desk can negotiate — and who approves it.

Write it on the registration card the guest signs.

Housekeeping turnaround

An hourly room must be cleaned and inspected before the next guest. Block a turnaround window — 45 to 60 minutes is typical for a standard room — between slots, and make sure housekeeping sees the departure the moment it happens. A phone-based housekeeping view that marks a room dirty at check-out and clean when done makes this practical. See housekeeping room-status workflow.

Compliance does not shrink with the stay

  • Collect guest ID as for any stay.
  • File Form-III within 24 hours for foreign nationals, even for a three-hour stay.
  • Issue a GST invoice with a sequential number. Hourly rates in small hotels generally fall in the 5% band; confirm your position with your accountant.

Where it goes wrong

  • Selling hourly stays off the books to avoid paperwork — the room shows vacant while occupied, and the revenue never reaches the accounts.
  • No live view of when each hourly guest is due out, so overstays are discovered by accident.
  • Night bookings colliding with an afternoon slot that ran late.

The fix for all three is the same: hourly stays need to live on the same room board as night stays, with their end time visible.

A worked example: one room, one day

A 30-room hotel near a railway station sells room 105 three times in 24 hours:

SlotTimePrice
6-hour slot8am – 2pm₹1,500
Turnaround2pm – 3pm—
3-hour slot3pm – 6pm₹1,000
Turnaround6pm – 7pm—
Night stayFrom 7pm₹2,400

Revenue from the room: ₹4,900 instead of ₹2,400, at the cost of two extra cleans. The arrangement only works if the front desk sees the slots and turnarounds on the room board, and housekeeping knows the moment each guest leaves.

Policies to write down

  • Slots offered and their prices, by day of week.
  • Check-in grace period and overstay charges.
  • ID requirements — identical to night stays.
  • Whether hourly stays can be booked in advance or only walk-in.
  • Maximum slots per room per day, to protect housekeeping quality.

Selling hourly stays

Most hourly business comes from people searching on the day. Mention hourly stays clearly on your Google Business Profile and website, with the slot prices; tell nearby businesses, hospitals and transport operators; and make sure the front desk answers the phone with the prices ready.

Common questions

How should a hotel price hourly stays?
Most hotels price fixed slots — 3, 6 and 12 hours, for example — as a proportion of the night rate, with a premium per hour on shorter slots to cover housekeeping. Keep a clear overstay rate and decide in advance when an overstay becomes a full night.
Do hourly stays need guest ID and registration?
Yes. Guest identification and registration rules, including Form-III for foreign nationals, apply regardless of how long the guest stays.
How is GST charged on hourly stays?
GST on accommodation depends on the value charged for the unit. Confirm with your accountant how your slot pricing maps to the ₹7,500 threshold; for most hourly rates in small hotels, the 5% band applies.
