eZee and Hotelogix alternatives for small hotels: a fair comparison.

We build a hotel management system, so we compete with eZee and Hotelogix. Read this with that in mind. We've tried to write the comparison a hotel owner would want: including the hotels for whom the established cloud platforms are simply the better choice.

Short answer

eZee Absolute and Hotelogix are established cloud PMS platforms with strong OTA channel managers, booking engines and integrations, and they suit hotels that sell heavily on OTAs or run several properties. Small Indian hotels look at alternatives mainly for pricing that does not rise per room, reliable offline operation, built-in Form-III and GST features, or simpler software. Compare on OTA needs, connectivity and five-year cost.

What the established platforms do well

eZee (eZee Technosys, whose cloud PMS is eZee Absolute) and Hotelogix are long-established hotel software companies with large installed bases in India and abroad. Their strengths:

  • Mature channel managers connected to the major OTAs, keeping rates and availability in sync.
  • Booking engines for direct bookings on the hotel's own website.
  • Cloud access from anywhere, and multi-property management.
  • Integrations with payment gateways, POS, accounting and more.

If you sell most of your rooms through several OTAs, run more than one property, or want a booking engine today, these platforms belong on your shortlist.

Why some small hotels look elsewhere

Per-room pricing at their size

Cloud PMS is usually priced per room per month, often with add-ons. For a 30 or 40-room property, five-year totals can be substantial. Run the numbers: per-room versus flat pricing.

Connectivity

A cloud PMS needs a working connection to check guests in. Properties in hill stations, coastal towns or anywhere with unreliable internet sometimes prefer a system that runs on the front-desk PC and keeps working offline.

Local compliance and operations

Indian small hotels need Form-III (C-Form) for foreign guests within 24 hours, GST invoices with the current room-tariff bands, GSTR-1 and Tally exports, and often staff payroll and an F&B till in the same system. Check exactly how each platform handles these for your property.

Simplicity

Platforms built for hotels of every size can carry more configuration than a 15-room property needs.

Questions to ask every vendor

  • What is my five-year total at my room count, with every module I need?
  • What exactly happens at check-in if the internet is down?
  • How does it handle Form-III and the 5%/18% room GST bands?
  • Is there a charge per device, per user or per login?
  • Can I export guests, bookings and invoices myself?

How to run a fair side-by-side trial

If you are choosing between an established cloud PMS and an alternative, trial them the same way:

  1. Load the same rooms, room types and rate plans into each.
  2. Enter the same week of real bookings, including a group and a foreign guest.
  3. Run the same check-in, room move, F&B charge, part payment and check-out in each.
  4. Unplug the internet and repeat the check-in.
  5. Generate the same month-end exports for your accountant.
  6. Have the same receptionist and housekeeper use each for a day.

Score each on speed, mistakes made, and how your accountant rates the exports. Price comes last, once you know which products can actually do the job.

Switching PMS without disruption

  • Export all future bookings from the old system before cancelling it.
  • Choose a quiet week for the switch, away from festivals and wedding season.
  • Run both systems in parallel for three to five days.
  • Reconcile the first week's revenue in both before trusting the new one alone.
  • Update OTA connections last, once the new system is stable.
  • Keep read access to the old system until your accountant has closed the year.

Five-year cost: an illustration

For a 30-room hotel at an illustrative ₹100 per room per month, a per-room subscription totals ₹1,80,000 over five years before add-ons; at ₹60 it is ₹1,08,000. A flat ₹12,000-a-year licence totals ₹60,000. Your actual quotes may differ substantially — the point is to compare five-year totals at your room count, including the channel manager and booking engine if you need them.

Common questions

What are the alternatives to eZee Absolute?
Other cloud PMS platforms; on-premise or desktop PMS that run on the hotel's own PC, such as Slate; and simpler tools for very small properties. The right choice depends on how much you sell through OTAs, how many properties you run and how reliable your internet is.
Is Hotelogix good for small hotels?
Hotelogix is a mature cloud PMS used by many hotels and offers OTA connectivity and a booking engine. Small hotels should compare the all-in price at their room count, how it behaves offline, and whether they need the modules included.
Which hotel software is cheaper than eZee or Hotelogix?
Prices vary by plan and room count, so compare written quotes. As a pattern, flat annual licences cost less over five years than per-room subscriptions for mid-size properties, while per-room plans can be cheaper for very small ones.
