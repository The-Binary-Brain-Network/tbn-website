2. Free trials

  • Folio, Pass and Slate each offer a 15-day free trial of the complete software. No payment details are taken.
  • One trial per product per computer. Trialling one product does not use up the trial of another.
  • Starting a trial needs an internet connection once. Folio starts its trial from inside the app; for Pass and Slate we issue the trial key on request.
  • When the trial ends the software stops at its licence screen until a paid key is entered. Your data stays on your computer and is not deleted.
  • Nothing is charged automatically at the end of a trial.

3. Licences and prices

  • Folio — ₹6,500 per property per year.
  • Pass — ₹7,500 per billing computer per year. Every outlet operated from that computer's installation is included.
  • Slate — ₹12,000 per property per year, not per room and not per device. Phones and tablets connecting to the licensed front-desk computer on the property's network are included.
  • Licences run for one year and are bound to the computer they are activated on.
  • Installation, onboarding and staff training are included. Updates released during the licence year are included.
  • Renewal is not automatic. We contact you before renewal; the renewal price is confirmed with you in advance and never changes during a licence term.

4. Referral credit

  • If a business you refer buys a paid licence for any TBN product, you — the referring customer — receive a ₹1,500 credit, applied immediately against your account rather than held until renewal.
  • Credits stack across successful referrals. The referred business pays the standard price, and can then earn referral credit of its own.
  • Credit is given once per referred business.

5. Your data

Your business data stays on your hardware and remains yours. Every product exports it in standard formats. Our handling of the limited data we do receive is described in the privacy policy.

The software helps you produce correct bills, tax documents and compliance records, but you remain responsible for the information you enter, the GST rates and registrations you configure, and your filings. Keep backups — each product makes them automatically — and keep copies off the machine for anything you cannot afford to lose.
