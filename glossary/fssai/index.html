FSSAI · Food Safety and Standards Authority of India

FSSAI — Food Safety and Standards Authority of India

FSSAI is the statutory body that licenses every food business operating in India. Every restaurant, hotel kitchen, caterer, food truck and packaged-food seller needs an FSSAI number — and it has to appear on your bills, your menu, and displayed on premises.

The three licence tiers

FSSAI issues three tiers of licence based on annual turnover and business scope. You cannot pick — it's determined by your numbers.

TierApplies toNumber Length
Basic RegistrationTurnover up to ₹12 lakh — small eateries, home-based sellers, tea stalls14 digits
State LicenceTurnover ₹12 lakh to ₹20 crore — most standalone restaurants and small chains14 digits
Central LicenceTurnover above ₹20 crore, imports, e-commerce sellers, chains operating in more than one state14 digits

Where the FSSAI number must appear

Compliance requires the number in all of the following places:

  • On the bill. Printed on every customer receipt.
  • On the menu. The physical menu card and any digital menu (website, QR-code menu, delivery app listings).
  • On packaging for any pre-packaged item you sell (bottled water, packaged snacks, take-home boxes with your branding).
  • Displayed on premises. A printed board with your FSSAI number, visible to customers.
  • On advertising. Any print or digital advertisement mentioning your food product.

Renewal cadence

Licences are valid for 1 to 5 years, at the licensee's option. Renewal must be filed at least 30 days before expiry — late renewal incurs a per-day penalty (₹100/day, capped at the licence fee), and running a food business on an expired FSSAI licence attracts a fine of up to ₹5 lakh under the Food Safety Act.

What the number looks like

An FSSAI number is 14 digits. The first digit encodes the licence type (1 = Central, 2 = State). Digits 2–6 are the year and state code. Digits 7–14 are a unique application ID. Example: 12345678901234. When printing on a bill, the standard format is "FSSAI: 12345 6789 01234" for readability.
